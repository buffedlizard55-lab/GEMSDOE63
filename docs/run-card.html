Hypothesis

The scoreable truth is concentrated in the annulus immediately around the mapped trace network, because (a) known USGS/INGENIOUS fault pixels are masked out of evaluation in both rounds [OFFICIAL, thread 11516], (b) a 'new fault' includes newly mapped geometry of an existing system [OFFICIAL, thread 11536], and (c) measured USGS trace displacement in north-central Nevada reaches 400 m = 4 px [Hermant et al., SGW 2025].

Mechanism

A hard ribbon of CVaR-selected width around the catalogue, with the catalogue pixels themselves removed, plus trace-tip continuations; the width and the retained distance bands are chosen by mean DTI penalised by the worst-tail average over an ensemble of shifted/omitted/decoyed label worlds.

The named non-fault process that could mimic it

A laterally displaced mapped trace is indistinguishable, in this geometry, from (i) a parallel splay or secondary strand of the same system, (ii) a scarp formed by landslide or alluvial-fan edge construction, and (iii) an anthropogenic linear feature (road cut, canal, pipeline). Only (i) is a fault; (ii) and (iii) are not, and nothing in this lane separates them.
