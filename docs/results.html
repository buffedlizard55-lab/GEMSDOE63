Why wider is worse

The per-band diagnostic (12 worlds per fold, same generator) is the cleanest statement of the mechanism. Reading down the table, every band out to 4 px has a mean realised kernel weight far above the naive marginal bar of 0.2 × DTI ≈ 0.045, so the naive rule would emit them all. The exact metric rejects them, because a displaced trace coincides with one band and every other band pays (1 - kmax) per pixel for it.
