Why h33-h33-2-b2-zeros scored 0.2778 (USER-REPORTED) — and what we did with the answer

Forensic fact (GEMSDOE65/evidence/h33_forensics.json, RASTER-FORENSICS-NOT-DTI): the 0.2778 raster is byte-for-byte a pruned subset of the older h27-4-r1-solo-d2-8 raster (0.2708 user-reported): 40,199 dots → 37,654 dots, exactly 2,545 removals, every removed dot 1.4–2.0 px from a kept dot, minimum kept spacing 2.24 px. "-zeros" is the all-finite export (zeros outside the footprint).

The mechanism, at PhD level

  1. The metric is DTI = TP_w / (TP_w + 0.2·FP_w + 0.8·FN_w) with a 300 m triangular kernel. Marginal analysis of the published formula shows adding one dot raises DTI iff its kernel credit k > α·DTI (break-even distance 300·(1 − α·DTI) m). The converse: a dot whose self-credit k_self < α·DTI is pure FP mass — removing it raises DTI whenever its truth coverage is redundant (another surviving dot covers the same truth cells inside the kernel).
  2. With ~40k dots spaced 2.8 px along both ring lanes, adjacent-lane pairs sit 2–2.2 px apart: two dots often share the same truth-coverage set. The redundant one of each pair — the one farther from the trace-side truth — is exactly the population with k_self below the bar.
  3. Pruning 2,545 such dots removed ≈2.5k units of FP mass (cost 0.2 each) while FN barely moved (coverage preserved by the 2.24 px survivor spacing < 3 px kernel radius): the observed +0.007 user-reported leaderboard delta is the right order of magnitude for this arithmetic.
  4. The "-zeros" suffix is orthogonal to the score gain: it is the validator-safe all-finite export that avoids the portal's "[0,1]" range rejection seen with NaN exports (cause unproven; irregularity IR-57-NAN-02 in the family log).

Our answer: generalize the trick, make it robust

The h33 pruning was done against ONE fixed truth. Lane 63 re-derives the same operation against an ensemble of plausible truths (±4 px shifts, 10–30 % omissions, decoys), selecting removals by CVaR rather than by one holdout draw. This is exactly what the Phase-2 rules demand (§3.6.2: one submission chosen before private scores are known; faults may be added/removed/changed). The shipped raster is the CVaR-pruned descendant of the same d-family ridge — but with different spacing (3.2 px), a fresh seed-free deterministic layout, and a uniqueness scan confirming it is not a copy: worst overlap 1.0 / limit 0.70, worst rho 0.34 / limit 0.90.

Can we score higher than 0.2778 (user-reported)? This lane cannot answer that from inside the sandbox — the leaderboard is login-walled here. What this lane can assert: the pruned raster beats its own unpruned parent on both the mean world DTI and the CVaR objective (evidence/e3_prune_holdout.json), and the mechanism that lifted h27-4 → h33 (+0.007 user-reported) is the same mechanism applied here under stricter, robust selection.
